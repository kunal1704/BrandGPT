# 🚀 BrandGPT – The AI Co-Creator for Personal Brands

**BrandGPT** is your always-on content strategist — powered by cutting-edge open-source LLMs and built for creators, founders, and businesses who need to communicate with clarity, creativity, and consistency.

From killer taglines to high-converting LinkedIn posts, BrandGPT crafts content that _sounds like you_, aligns with your mission, and actually connects with your audience.

---

## 🔥 Why BrandGPT?

| 💡 Built for                  | 🛠️ Powered by                                                      |
| ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Personal Brands & Creators    | Mistral-7B-Instruct & or Llama 3 8B both => (local, fast, private) |
| Solo Founders & Startup Teams | Ollama + FastAPI backend                                           |
| Content Agencies & Marketers  | Scalable architecture ready for SaaS                               |

---

## ✨ What It Can Do

- 🧠 **Define Your Brand** – Set tone, voice, audience, and vibe
- ✍️ **Generate Content Instantly** – Tweets, bios, hooks, posts, captions — all in your tone
- 🎨 **Customize the Style** – Casual, witty, professional, punchy, Gen-Z, poetic, technical
- 🔁 **Iterate Fast** – Rephrase, remix, and generate multiple variations on the fly
- 🔒 **Local + Private** – Powered by quantized LLMs running locally — no data leaks

---

## 🛠️ Built With

- **LLM**: `Mistral-7B-Instruct` (GGUF via `llama.cpp` / `Ollama`) & `Llama-3-8B-Quantized` (GPTQ via `Hugging Face`)
- **Backend**: FastAPI (cleanly modular, API-ready)
- **Frontend**: Next.js 14 + TailwindCSS (Aceternity UI, animations ready)
- **Infra**: Fully local, but designed to be deployed anywhere

---

## 🧪 Current Status
